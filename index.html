About Us

R Noone Plastering is a trusted construction and home improvement specialist based in Donegal, Ireland. With a focus on quality workmanship and customer satisfaction, we cover all aspects of plastering for both residential and commercial projects.

Whether it's a small repair or a large renovation, we are dedicated to delivering a flawless finish. We pride ourselves on being reliable, efficient, and professional.

Our Services

Skimming

Achieve a perfectly smooth surface for your walls and ceilings, ready for painting or wallpapering. We ensure a high-quality finish every time.

Rendering

High-quality internal and external rendering services to protect your property and enhance its appearance. We use durable materials for long-lasting results.

Dry Lining

Professional dry lining services for improved insulation and a clean, modern finish. An efficient way to finish internal walls and ceilings.

Renovations

Comprehensive plastering solutions for home renovations and extensions. We work closely with you to bring your vision to life.
